About me

Researcher · Educator · Collaborator

I am an Assistant Researcher at Ulsan National Institute of Science and Technology, working at the intersection of MRI analysis and sensor-based diagnosis of Movement Disorders. I develops deep learning methods — from CNN-based segmentation to image synthesis and wearable-signal modelling — to support early, objective and accessible diagnosis.
